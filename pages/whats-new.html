Latest highlights

Recent updates include:

  • Shaktipeeth browse and center pages show localized names, cities, state labels, and about text in your app language.
  • Event titles and news on Shaktipeeth center tabs are localized where translations are available.
  • Library → Magazines “Recent” opens the latest year with issues ready to read (instead of a blank current year).
  • Ritual steps let you select and copy mantra or explanation text—for example to share on WhatsApp.
  • Dhyan And Sadhna playlist under Audio → Playlists — Gurudev meditation and sadhana in his voice.
  • Maha Shivratri and Raksha Bandhan guidance under Rituals → Karmkand → Hindu Festivals.
  • Ritual settings on the Rituals tab — script, text size, layout, and Gayatri repeats.
  • Better layout on iPad and larger screens (covers and Audio Speed Dial stay readable).
  • Offline downloads for audio and clearer Library browsing.
  • CarPlay browsing with cover art, plus audio search.
  • Cleaner Daily Thought sharing on WhatsApp (preview card without duplicated text).
  • Hindi Library lists reliable book downloads (broken Archive.org-only links removed from the downloadable list).
  • Library filters live on their own page — tap the language chip next to search.
  • USA and Canada Shaktipeeth list now includes more centers and local contacts.
  • Panchang now shows the Hindu month and today’s / upcoming festivals.
  • If you skipped morning thought reminders, Home may ask again after a few days. You can still say not now.
  • On festival days like Janmashtami or Raksha Bandhan, Home Panchang offers a short ritual or share action.
  • Major AWGP establishments (Shantikunj, Tapobhumi, Awalkheda, DSVV, and more) are now in the Shaktipeeth directory with addresses.
Your installed version is listed under Profile → App Version. Store build numbers can differ slightly between iOS and Android.

How to update

Updates ship through the App Store and Google Play. Enable automatic updates, or search “AWGP App” and tap Update. If Profile shows an update is available, you can open the store listing from there.

Share feedback

Use Profile → Submit Feedback to tell us what works and what doesn’t. Device and app version are included automatically.
