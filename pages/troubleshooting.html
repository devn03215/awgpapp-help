🌩️ Troubleshooting

Audio won't play

  1. Check volume and silent mode / focus settings on your device.
  2. Switch Wi‑Fi and cellular to rule out network blocks.
  3. Force-quit AWGP and reopen the track.
  4. Update to the latest App Store or Play Store version.
  5. If using Bluetooth or CarPlay, disconnect and reconnect once.

Downloads & storage

If a download fails, ensure you have free space (Profile → Storage). Delete old downloads you no longer need, then retry on Wi‑Fi.

Large books or magazines may take several minutes on slow connections — keep the app in the foreground during the first download.

Notifications not arriving

  1. iOS/Android Settings → AWGP → Notifications → Allow.
  2. Confirm reminder is enabled inside AWGP Profile.
  3. Disable battery saver restrictions for AWGP on Android.
  4. On iOS, Scheduled Summary and Focus modes can delay alerts.

Account registration (not available yet)

AWGP does not offer sign-up or sign-in in the current release. If you see old screenshots or docs mentioning accounts, ignore them for now — onboarding is still in development.

When registration opens in a future version, this page will include troubleshooting for email and phone sign-in. Until then, use the app without an account; your data stays on your device.

Send feedback or report a bug

Profile → Send feedback attaches app version and device info automatically. Describe what you expected, what happened, and whether it reproduces every time.

You can also email devn03215@gmail.com or use Contact support.
