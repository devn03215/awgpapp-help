📚 Library

Books & EPUB reader Magazines & Akhandjyoti Audiobooks & podcasts Reading lists & offline Share a book or magazine link

Books & EPUB reader

The Library tab lists VicharKranti and related books. Tap a title for details, then open the reader. EPUB books support adjustable typography, themes, and progress saved on your device.

Switch between grid and list view using the icon in the Library header.

Magazines & Akhandjyoti

Browse Akhandjyoti and other magazines from Library. Issues open in a dedicated reader with page navigation and reading settings.

Audiobooks & podcasts

Audiobooks and podcasts have their own sections with chapter or episode lists. Playback continues in the background like other audio in AWGP.

Reading lists & offline

Save items to your reading list from a book or magazine detail screen. Downloaded files are stored on device — check Profile → Storage for usage and cleanup.

Share a book or magazine link

Use the share action on a title to send a link. Friends see a preview card and can open the item in AWGP after installing the app.
