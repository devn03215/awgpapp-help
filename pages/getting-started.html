Welcome to AWGP App

AWGP App brings All World Gayatri Pariwar content to your phone: Pragya Geet and bhajans, daily rituals, VicharKranti books, Akhandjyoti magazines, Gayatri Japa, Panchang, Daily Thought, and local Shaktipeeth events — in one calm, focused app.

Open AWGP App and start exploring — no account or sign-up is required for browsing content in the current release. Hosting events for a center uses invite-only organizer access after review.

Install on iOS & Android

Download AWGP App from your device’s app store:

App Store (iOS) Google Play (Android)

Or open our install page for a QR code, copy link, or printable poster.

Shared links from WhatsApp or SMS open a web page first. Tap Open in AWGP App after the app is installed.

No account needed

Browsing audio, rituals, library, and published events works without creating an account. Progress such as reading position, downloads, and Interested/Going choices stays on your device.

  • Install and open AWGP App — browse right away.
  • Organizer hosting — apply from a center; if approved you get an invite email (not open public signup).
  • Profile — notifications, appearance, storage, feedback, and help.
See Events & Shaktipeeths for finding centers, RSVPs, and hosting.

Navigating the app

The bottom tabs are your home base:

  • Home — Panchang teaser, quick actions, continue listening/reading.
  • Rituals — Yagya flows and Gayatri Japa.
  • Library — Books, magazines, audiobooks, and podcasts.
  • Shaktipeeths — Centers on a list or map, events, and organizer tools.
  • Audio — Mantras, Pragya Geet, aarti, and playlists.
  • Profile — Settings, notifications, feedback, and help.

System requirements

  • iOS: iPhone or iPad on a recent iOS version supported by the App Store listing.
  • Android: Phone or tablet with Google Play; edge-to-edge display supported.
  • Network: Internet required for streaming and first-time downloads; some content can be saved offline.
  • CarPlay (iOS): Available on supported builds for in-car audio.
