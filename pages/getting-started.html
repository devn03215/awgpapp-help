Welcome to AWGP App

AWGP App brings All World Gayatri Pariwar content to your phone: Pragya Geet and bhajans, daily rituals, VicharKranti books, Akhandjyoti magazines, Gayatri Japa, Panchang, and Daily Thought — in one calm, focused app.

Open AWGP App and start exploring — no account or sign-up is required in the current release.

Install on iOS & Android

Download AWGP App from your device’s app store:

App Store (iOS) Google Play (Android)
Shared links from WhatsApp or SMS open a web page first. Tap Open in AWGP App after the app is installed.

No account needed

User registration and onboarding are not open yet. The current app is meant to be used without creating an account.

  • Install and open AWGP App — browse audio, rituals, and library right away.
  • Progress stays on your device — reading position, downloads, and settings are saved locally.
  • Profile — notifications, appearance, storage, feedback, and help (no sign-in screen today).
Sign-in and cloud sync may arrive in a future update. This help center will be updated when they launch.

Navigating the app

The bottom tabs are your home base:

  • Home — Panchang teaser, quick actions, continue listening/reading.
  • Rituals — Yagya flows and Gayatri Japa.
  • Library — Books, magazines, audiobooks, and podcasts.
  • Audio — Mantras, Pragya Geet, aarti, and playlists.
  • Profile — Settings, notifications, feedback, and help.

System requirements

  • iOS: iPhone or iPad on a recent iOS version supported by the App Store listing.
  • Android: Phone or tablet with Google Play; edge-to-edge display supported.
  • Network: Internet required for streaming and first-time downloads; some content can be saved offline.
  • CarPlay (iOS): Available on supported builds for in-car audio.
