Is AWGP App free?

AWGP App is free to download and use. Content is provided by All World Gayatri Pariwar for sadhana and study. Store listings and future offerings may change; check the app stores for current details.

Relationship to awgp.org

AWGP App is a companion to awgp.org. Books, audio, and magazines align with AWGP publications. The website remains the source for organizational news and deeper web resources.

Languages & content

The library includes multiple languages (for example Hindi, Gujarati, and English titles where available). Audio and rituals may vary by language — browse each section for what is published today. New content is added over time without requiring a separate app install.

Can anyone host events?

Anyone can browse published events and mark Interested or Going. Creating events for a Shaktipeeth requires organizer access: apply from the center page, wait for AWGP review, then accept an invite email. New events stay pending until an editor publishes them. See Become an organizer and Create an event.

Apple Watch companion

A minimal Apple Watch app (Now Playing and Daily Thought) is rolling out on supported iOS builds. It requires a recent iPhone build and Apple Watch paired to the same Apple ID. Android does not have a watch companion.

Android vs iOS features

Both platforms support core audio, rituals, and library features. iOS additionally offers CarPlay and Apple Watch in supported builds. UI follows platform conventions (edge-to-edge on Android, safe areas on iOS).

How to update the app

Updates ship through the App Store and Google Play. Enable automatic updates or search “AWGP App” in your store and tap Update. Note your version under Profile when reporting issues.
