Playing audio tracks

Open the Audio tab to browse Mantra, Playlists, Pragya Geet, and Aarti. Tap any track to open the full player with artwork, progress, and lyrics (when available).

Use the segment control at the top to jump between categories. Pragya Geet includes a Speed dial carousel for quick access to favorites.

Gayatri Mantra & aarti

The Gayatri Mantra track includes extra context and classification options. Aarti tracks are grouped under the Aarti section for evening prayers and chalisa.

Playlists (private & public)

  • Private playlists — created on your device under Audio → Playlists → Private. Great for personal sadhana sets.
  • Public playlists — curated collections shared with all users.

From the full player, use Add to playlist to save a track to a private list.

Mini player & CarPlay

When you leave the Audio tab while something is playing, a mini player appears above the tab bar. Tap it to return to the full player.

On iOS, AWGP supports CarPlay for audio playback in supported builds. Connect your iPhone to a CarPlay-enabled vehicle or simulator.

Sharing audio

Tap the share icon on a track to send a link via WhatsApp, Messages, or email. Recipients see a preview and can open the track in AWGP if they have the app installed.
