Daily Thought

Each day AWGP surfaces a thought from Gurudev’s teachings (and optional custom thoughts you add). Open it from Home or the dedicated Daily Thought screen. Share as an image card via the share sheet.

Filter categories and topics under Profile → Daily Thought settings to personalize what appears in your rotation.

Daily Thought reminders

Enable a daily notification from Profile:

  1. Open Profile → Notifications (or Daily Thought settings).
  2. Turn on Daily Thought reminder.
  3. Pick time and repeat days.
  4. Allow notifications when iOS or Android prompts you.
If reminders stop after an OS update, open AWGP once and confirm notification permission is still enabled in system Settings.

Panchang on Home

Home shows a Panchang teaser with tithi, nakshatra, and key timings for your location. Tap through for the full Panchang screen and deeper calendar details.

My location for Panchang

Accurate Panchang depends on your location. Set it under Profile → My location or when prompted from Home. You can search for a city or use GPS where available.
