Find Shaktipeeths & centers

Open the Shaktipeeths tab to explore AWGP centers near you or around the world. You can switch between list and map views (Android map needs a Google Maps key in your build; iOS uses Apple Maps).

  • Tap a center to open its detail page — overview, contact, and upcoming events.
  • From a center you follow or organize, you may also open My Shaktipeeth for a home-style view of that center’s events.

Browse & open events

Published events appear on the center page and in My Shaktipeeth under Events. Each card shows the title, date/time, venue, and price when available.

Tap an event for the full detail screen: flyer or cover, description, location, organizer contact, recurrence (one-time, weekly, or monthly), and response buttons.

Only published events are shown to everyone. New events stay hidden until an AWGP editor approves them (see Approval & publishing).

Interested & Going

On a published event, you can tap Interested or Going. The app shows public totals only (how many people marked each option).

  • Privacy: your personal choice is stored on this device. We do not publish your name or require an account for these counts.
  • Totals update when you change your response; tapping the same option again clears it.
  • Clearing app data (Profile → clear local data) removes your saved responses on that device.

Share an event

Use the share icon on an event to send a link. Recipients open a web page first, then can tap Open in AWGP App if the app is installed.

Become an organizer

Hosting events for a center is invite-only after review. Anyone can apply from a center’s page with Become organizer (name and email required; phone and a short message are optional).

  1. Open the center → Become organizer.
  2. Submit your application. You will see a pending status until it is reviewed.
  3. If approved, you receive an email invite to set up hosting access for that center.
  4. After you accept the invite and sign in, you can create events for that Shaktipeeth.
Public self-serve “sign up to host” is closed. Applying does not grant hosting until an editor approves you and sends an invite.

Create an event

Organizers (and center editors) see Create event on the center or My Shaktipeeth screen.

  • Enter a title (required), description, start time, optional end time, recurrence, and contact info.
  • Use start time format YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM (24-hour).
  • Submit the event — it is saved as pending and is not public yet.

You may see a pending badge on the event until an AWGP editor publishes it. After publishing, it appears in upcoming lists for all visitors.

My Shaktipeeth

My Shaktipeeth is a focused home for a center you organize or follow closely. Use the segments:

  • Events — upcoming list and create/share actions when you have hosting access.
  • Center — center details (placeholder sections may expand over time).
  • News — reserved for future center updates.

Approval & publishing

Two separate reviews keep quality and safety high:

  • Organizer applications — reviewed by AWGP; approved applicants get an invite email.
  • Events — reviewed after you create them; only published events are listed publicly.

If your application or event seems stuck on pending, wait for email from the AWGP team, or contact support from the app (Profile → Feedback) or the Help Center contact page.
