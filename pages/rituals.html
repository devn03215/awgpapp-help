Rituals overview

The Rituals tab guides you through daily spiritual practice — from Gayatri Japa to structured Yagya sessions. Each ritual opens step-by-step with audio cues where applicable.

Gayatri Japa counter

Gayatri Japa helps you count repetitions with a calm, focused interface. Progress can appear on Home under Continue so you can resume your session.

Use headphones or speaker at a comfortable volume. The counter works offline once the screen is loaded.

Yagya practices

AWGP offers several Yagya formats:

  • Quick / abridged — shorter daily practice.
  • Full Yagya — complete guided flow.
  • Custom Yagya — build or open saved configurations.

Choose the version that fits your schedule; you can always switch from the Rituals menu.

Continue where you left off

Home shows a Continue carousel when you have in-progress rituals, books, or audio. Swipe to dismiss an item you no longer need.
