Using AWGP App today

AWGP App does not offer user registration or sign-in yet. Onboarding and account creation are still in development and are not available in the current app release.

You can use the full app without an account. Reading progress, playlists, downloads, reminders, and preferences are stored on your device unless we say otherwise in a future update.

Manage appearance (light/dark/system), notifications, storage, and other options from the Profile tab. When accounts launch in a later version, we will update this help center with sign-up and sign-in instructions.

Privacy & your data

Today, AWGP App mainly keeps data on your phone or tablet — for example reading position, downloaded files, ritual progress, and reminder settings. If you send feedback through the app, we receive what you write plus basic device and app version details to help us fix issues.

We do not sell your personal information. Read the full policy: Privacy Policy (also available from Profile → Your data when shown in the app).

Anonymous crash reports

Under Profile → Application → Anonymous crash reports you can optionally allow AWGP to receive a short technical report when the app crashes. This setting is off by default.

When enabled, a report may include:

  • Error message and a scrubbed stack trace
  • App version and build number
  • Device OS name and version

It does not include your books, reading progress, playlists, downloads, account content, or other personal library data. Emails, tokens, and URL query strings are removed before anything is stored or sent.

Reports are stored on device first and only uploaded if you turn the setting on. You can turn it off again anytime from the same Profile toggle.

Notification settings

Control reminders from Profile:

  • Daily Thought
  • Book and magazine reading reminders
  • System permission must be allowed for any alert to appear

These settings are stored locally on your device.

Clear local data

To free space or remove downloads, open Profile → Manage Storage and delete items you no longer need. Uninstalling AWGP App removes app data from your device.

If you previously sent feedback that included personal information and want it removed, email devn03215@gmail.com with enough detail for us to find your message.

Account-based data deletion will apply once user registration is available. Until then, clearing local storage or uninstalling the app is how to remove on-device data.
