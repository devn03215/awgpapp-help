Using AWGP App today

AWGP App does not offer public user registration or sign-in in the current release. You can use the full app without creating an account.

Reading progress, playlists, downloads, reminders, and preferences are stored on your device unless we say otherwise in a future update.

Manage appearance (light/dark/system), notifications, storage, and other options from the Profile tab. If account sign-in becomes generally available later, we will update this Help Center with sign-up and sign-in steps.

Privacy & your data

Most day-to-day data stays on your phone or tablet — for example reading position, downloaded files, ritual progress, and reminder settings.

When you send feedback, or if you optionally enable anonymous crash reports, we may receive what you write plus limited app and device details (such as OS version, app version, and technical identifiers needed to deliver notifications or support) so we can fix issues. App libraries and services we use to run the app may process device or other IDs only for app functionality — not for advertising or sale.

We do not sell your personal information. Read the full policy: Privacy Policy (also available from Profile → Your data when shown in the app).

Request data deletion

AWGP App (Google Play / App Store listing). Use the steps below to request deletion of data we may hold on our servers.

  1. Email devn03215@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request — AWGP App.
  2. Include enough detail for us to find your data (for example the approximate date you sent feedback, or text from the message). Do not send passwords.
  3. We will delete feedback and optional crash reports associated with your request from our servers within a reasonable period (typically within 30 days).

What we delete on request: feedback you submitted and anonymous crash reports you opted into (when we can identify them from your email).

What stays on your device only until you clear it: reading progress, downloads, playlists, reminders, and local preferences. Remove those via Profile → Manage Storage, or by uninstalling AWGP App. Uninstalling removes on-device app data.

Full policy: Privacy Policy — Data deletion.

Anonymous crash reports

Under Profile → Application → Anonymous crash reports you can optionally allow AWGP to receive a short technical report when the app crashes. This setting is off by default.

When enabled, a report may include:

  • Error message and a scrubbed stack trace
  • App version and build number
  • Device OS name and version

It does not include your books, reading progress, playlists, downloads, account content, or other personal library data. Emails, tokens, and URL query strings are removed before anything is stored or sent.

Reports are stored on device first and only uploaded if you turn the setting on. You can turn it off again anytime from the same Profile toggle.

Notification settings

Control reminders from Profile:

  • Daily Thought
  • Book and magazine reading reminders
  • System permission must be allowed for any alert to appear

These settings are stored locally on your device.

Clear local data

To free space or remove downloads, open Profile → Manage Storage and delete items you no longer need. Uninstalling AWGP App removes app data from your device.

For data that may already be on our servers (feedback or crash reports), use Request data deletion above.
